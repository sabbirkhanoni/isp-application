# bKash Merchant Support — Questionnaire (spike S2)

Send to bKash merchant support (email / merchant hotline / relationship manager) and keep the written answers. Ask the ISP owner to send it from the account that holds the merchant agreement.

**Context to state:** We are an internet service provider in Bangladesh. Our customers pay monthly bills to our bKash Merchant account using the app's "Merchant Payment" option, and we want our billing software to confirm each payment automatically.

## Questions

1. Our account type is: (Merchant Payment / Payment Gateway / other). Which products are available to us?
2. When a customer pays our merchant number manually from their bKash app, can we receive a real-time notification (IPN / webhook) for that payment?
3. Can the customer enter a **reference** (e.g. their Customer ID) in that manual flow, and is the reference included in the notification and in the statement?
4. Is there a server-to-server API to verify a transaction by TrxID (amount, status, sender number, time)? Is it available for our account type?
5. What are the onboarding steps, documents, approval time and any fees/commission for API or checkout access?
6. Can we download the **merchant statement** as CSV/Excel (daily, monthly)? Which columns does it contain (TrxID, amount, sender, reference, time, balance)?
7. How are reversals/refunds shown, and how do we get notified?
8. Are there rules or restrictions on forwarding the merchant's SMS notifications to our own server for reconciliation?
9. Are there rate limits, IP allow-listing requirements or uptime/retry behaviour for webhooks?
10. Who is our technical contact for integration questions?
